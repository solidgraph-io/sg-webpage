About Us

We're Developers Who Build From Scratch — Not a Template Agency

SolidGraph Solutions LLC — custom website development for local businesses in Charlotte and Springfield.

We started SolidGraph Solutions because we kept seeing the same problem: local business owners were either stuck with a cheap template site that didn't represent them, or they'd been quoted outrageous prices by agencies that just reskinned the same WordPress theme for every client.

We build differently. Every website we create is written from scratch — custom code, custom design, built specifically for your business. No templates, no drag-and-drop builders, no shortcuts. The result is a faster, sharper, more professional website that you own completely.

We're a lean, senior-level team — which means lower costs for you and faster delivery than traditional agencies. We use modern tools and AI-assisted development to build high-quality custom websites at a fraction of what large agencies charge, without compromising on quality or craftsmanship.
